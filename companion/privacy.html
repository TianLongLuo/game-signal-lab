伴侣模式隐私说明

版本：2026-09-06-companion-v1。适用于虚构成年角色的「相伴、回忆、世界」，旧版关系记录独立保留。

哪些内容会保存？

启用前需要明确同意。确认的人物设定、已完成对话、分支选择、保留的记忆及生成插画会存入你的账号专属服务端空间。旧版对象档案不会自动作为人物或故事素材。录音只有在你主动录制并结束后才提交转写；文字草稿必须点击发送才进入故事。

哪些服务会处理内容？

DeepSeek 接收本次输入、必要的人物设定、近期故事和相关记忆。WAV 录音仅由部署的本地 FunASR 识别，失败直接返回错误，不向云端发送录音；句读整理使用 DeepSeek。仅点击生成插画时，外貌或世界描述才会发送给单独配置的图像服务（当前适配 OpenAI Images）。这些服务适用各自的数据处理条款。网站不声称能控制上游服务的保留周期。

谁可以访问？

网页 API 按登录账号隔离故事、记忆、向量检索和图片，图片不使用公开分享 URL。管理员旧版会话查看功能不包含伴侣对话。应用主密钥加密文字记录；图像和向量库须通过私有存储保护。服务器运营人员仍具有基础设施访问权限，这不是端到端加密或运营方零访问系统。

撤回、修改与删除

在设置中撤回同意会停止新的生成并取消待处理任务，不会自动删除你已经保存的故事。回忆可编辑、删除；删除记忆后，其来源回合不再进入后续模型上下文，但仍可在自己的历史中查看。删除整个故事会移除账号中的设定、对话、记忆、任务和图片。向量库清理由后台重试完成；服务中断时可能延迟。运营者备份不会自动同步删除，应向网站运营者确认实际备份保留周期。

统计与联系方式

此版伴侣界面不加载 GA4；不向分析服务发送剧情、录音或用户名。额度计数和任务状态属于运行所需信息。账户或备份删除问题请联系向你提供此网站试用的运营者；正式公开运营前应公布有效联系渠道和备份保留周期。

Companion privacy

Version: 2026-09-06-companion-v1. This mode is for fictional adult characters. Legacy relationship records stay separate.

Storage and processing

After explicit consent, confirmed character settings, completed conversations, choices, saved memories and generated art are stored privately for your account on the server. Legacy files are not automatically used. Recording is submitted for transcription when you finish it; its text remains an editable draft until you press Send.

DeepSeek receives your input and the character, recent story and relevant memories needed to respond. Only local FunASR handles audio; failures return an error with no cloud fallback. DeepSeek cleans up punctuation. Image descriptions go to the configured image provider (currently the OpenAI Images adapter) only when you request artwork. Each provider has its own data terms and retention practices.

Access and deletion

Story, memory, vector retrieval and image APIs are scoped to your signed-in account. Images are not public share links. Legacy admin conversation views do not include companion dialogue. Text is encrypted with the application key; infrastructure operators still have server access. This is not end-to-end encryption.

Withdraw consent in Settings to stop new generation and cancel pending work. Existing stories stay available for reading and deletion. Deleting a memory excludes its source conversation pair from subsequent model context while keeping your visible history. Deleting the story removes its live database records and images; vector cleanup may be delayed during outages. Backups follow the operator’s published retention period, not immediate automatic deletion.

This companion version does not load GA4 or send story content to analytics. Contact the operator who invited you for account or backup deletion. The operator must publish a valid contact channel and actual backup retention policy before a public launch.
