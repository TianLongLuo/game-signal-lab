OPTIONAL FIELD NOTES · 非必读攻略

把相处当作对话，
而不是一道解题。

这些简短练习供玩家自愿参考，不影响剧情概率、额度或结局。游戏人物与剧情均为虚构，剧情状态也不是现实中的“人心评分”。

  1. 说具体的话，也听具体的回答

    与其猜测“她是不是讨厌我”，先说“这次约定取消了，我有一点失落”。询问对方的想法，而不是替对方下结论。

  2. 介绍真实的自己

    分享你的兴趣、经历和感受。好奇心可以来自真实的个性，不必靠编造经历、制造嫉妒或忽冷忽热。

  3. 邀请要容易接受，也容易拒绝

    给出具体、低压力的提议，比如“周末想一起喝杯咖啡吗？不方便也没关系”。一次拒绝就是这次的答案；沉默与犹豫不是同意。

  4. 情绪是信息，不是控制工具

    允许紧张、开心与失落存在。情绪强烈时先暂停、照顾自己，不故意激怒、羞辱或用失联换取对方让步。

  5. 分歧可以谈，边界需要尊重

    先复述你听到的顾虑，再表达自己的需要。亲密接触需要明确、持续、可撤回的同意；“停一下”意味着现在停下。

  6. 共同回忆不是欠条

    一起经历新事物可以增加了解，但时间、礼物和金钱都不会让任何人欠下感情或身体上的回应。

  7. 关系可以修复，也可以结束

    和解需要双方愿意，不等于抹掉问题。分手可以让人难过，但不代表谁的价值被判定，也不要求继续追逐。

  8. 故事之外，保留自己的生活

    虚拟陪伴可以是娱乐与表达的空间。休息、朋友、兴趣和现实生活同样值得安排；离开游戏不会成为惩罚剧情的依据。

Connection is a conversation,
not a puzzle to solve.

Optional notes, not a winning strategy. Reading them does not change your story odds, allowance or ending. Characters are fictional, and story states are not a way to score real people.

  1. Be specific. Stay curious.

    Instead of assuming “They must dislike me,” try “I felt disappointed when our plan changed.” Ask what happened and leave room for their answer.

  2. Let someone meet the real you.

    Share interests, experiences and feelings you actually have. You do not need invented status, jealousy or hot-and-cold behavior to be interesting.

  3. Make invitations easy to decline.

    Suggest something clear and low-pressure: “Would you like to get coffee this weekend? No worries if not.” A no is an answer. Silence and hesitation are not consent.

  4. Feelings are information, not leverage.

    Excitement, nerves and disappointment are all allowed. Take a break when overwhelmed; do not provoke, shame or withdraw contact to force agreement.

  5. Talk through differences. Respect boundaries.

    Reflect the concern you heard before explaining your needs. Physical intimacy requires clear, ongoing and reversible consent. “Stop” means stop now.

  6. Shared memories are not a debt.

    New experiences can help you get to know each other. Time, gifts and money never entitle anyone to affection or intimacy.

  7. Repair is possible. So is an ending.

    Making up takes willingness on both sides, not pretending the issue disappeared. A breakup can hurt without being a verdict on your worth or a challenge to keep pursuing.

  8. Keep a life beyond the story.

    Fictional companionship can be a place to play and express yourself. Make room for rest, friends and interests too. Taking time away never triggers a punishment event.
