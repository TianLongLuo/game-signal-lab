How to Tell If Someone Is Interested or Just Being Polite

2026-08-03 · GAME Signal Lab Blog · 中文版

This is one of the classic puzzles of adult social life. Someone smiled at you — was that interest, or do they just smile at everyone? They agreed to your invitation — was that a genuine yes, or did they feel too awkward to decline?

A single behavior is never enough

Any single social behavior can't serve as reliable evidence. Someone replies fast — maybe they happened to be holding their phone. Someone laughs at your joke — maybe you're genuinely funny, and it has nothing to do with attraction. A single behavior has too many possible explanations. You need a pattern.

What to look for

These behaviors carry more weight than single signals: Do they initiate contact on their own? Do they remember details you mentioned before? Do they treat plans with you as a real commitment? Do they offer alternatives when they cancel? Especially — do they reach out when there's no practical reason to?

Another valuable signal: Do they interact with you outside of group settings? If all your exchanges happen with mutual friends present, it's far more likely to be ordinary social friendliness, not romantic interest.

The most reliable test

Offer a low-pressure, no-cost way to decline. For example: "If you're free this weekend, want to grab coffee? Totally fine if you're busy." A genuinely interested person will accept or propose an alternative time. A merely polite person may accept and cancel later — or not respond at all.
